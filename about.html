My Creative Journey

Welcome to my magical corner! I'm Hind, a Computer Science graduate specializing in Software Engineering, with a deep passion for UI/UX design and digital/traditional art.

Art has always been my way of expressing emotions and capturing details—from realistic pencil sketching and cross-hatching to digital illustration. DreamWings is a reflection of my love for warmth, storytelling, and magical aesthetics, built to inspire young hearts and minds.
